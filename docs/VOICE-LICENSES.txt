Voice transcription components
==============================

Parakeet TDT 0.6B V3 by NVIDIA
https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
The optional INT8 ONNX conversion is downloaded from Handy's model distribution.
Model weights are not included in this repository.

ParakeetSpeech.cs adapts greedy decoding from transcribe-rs:
https://github.com/cjpais/transcribe-rs/blob/efc66111d519a434e28d51d94740008a7ce29360/src/onnx/parakeet/mod.rs
The C# adaptation omits timestamp generation and adds cancellation and shared CPU sessions.
